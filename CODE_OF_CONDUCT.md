# Code of conduct

This applies everywhere in this repository: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commit messages.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to the SDKs, to networking or to English.
- Assume good intent, and criticise ideas, not people.
- Keep to the topic: the Erdigo integration protocol, its SDKs and what you build with them.
- Respect privacy, your own and other people's.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats or discrimination of any kind
- Sexual or violent content
- Spam, advertising, or posts made only to promote something
- Someone's personal information, such as their address, location or contact details, posted without their permission
- Asking for, or sharing, ways to get into someone else's devices, maps or data

## Reporting

Use **Report content** in the menu of the post or comment, and choose to report it to the repository maintainers.
Security problems are different: report them privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or delete posts and comments, move discussions, and block accounts that break these
rules. They usually warn first, unless the content puts someone at risk.
